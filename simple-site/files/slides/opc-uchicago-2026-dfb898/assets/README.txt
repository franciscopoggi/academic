Logo files used by the Mannheim theme (data-theme="mannheim"):

  logo.png             University of Mannheim — top-right of every content slide
  logo-white.png       Department of Economics, white — title slide (dark background)
  logo-department.png  Department of Economics, colour — not used by default;
                       to show it top-right instead, set in style.css:
                       --logo-src:url(assets/logo-department.png);

All three are trimmed, transparent PNGs made from the official files in
"University Logos/" (which the template does not read — that folder can go).
A missing file simply shows nothing. For another institution set --logo-src /
--logo-dark-src in style.css to your own files.
